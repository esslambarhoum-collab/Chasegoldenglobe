Our Origin

A trade in craftsmanship, before it was a trade in capital.

In 2006, Chase Golden Globe began by sourcing and exporting fine furniture out of Indonesia, furnishing hotels, restaurants and private homes across the globe. It was a business built on judgment: knowing quality, knowing markets, and knowing who to trust on the other side of a shipping container.

Today

Investors on one side. Projects on the other.

Fouad Barhoum now travels the world connecting investors to projects, acting as the middleman. He has worked on deals worth billions of dollars and helped raise significant capital, doing the due diligence, preparing the reports investors need, and building the pitch decks along the way.
