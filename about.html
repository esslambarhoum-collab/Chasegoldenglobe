About Chase Golden Globe.

A private Australian investment and trade house, built on two decades of relationships.

Who We Are

Your Vision, Our Capital.

Chase Golden Globe is a private Australian investment and trade facilitator. We connect developers, investors and institutional partners to long-term capital, and have done so since 2006 across nine markets worldwide.

About Us

Two Decades of Relationships

Founded in 2006, Chase Golden Globe began in construction and hospitality trade and grew outward from there. The network of partners, regulators and financiers built along the way is what we bring to every deal today.

Our Vision

The Firm You Call First

To be the first call when a deal needs to move quickly and cross a border, and to leave every market we work in better than we found it.

Our Mission

Bankable, Not Just Pitched

We build the financing structures that get projects funded — understanding the regulation, knowing who to call, and staying with a project until it is genuinely bankable.

A Note on Licensing

Chase Golden Globe is a private trading and advisory house — it is not itself a licensed financial services provider. Where a piece of work requires a specific professional licence, we work in partnership with appropriately licensed practitioners and firms to deliver it. See our Legal & Privacy page for details.
